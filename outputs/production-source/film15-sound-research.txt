Selection
MOSS-SoundEffect v2.0 is the selected sound-effects generator. Released 26 May 2026, its official model card describes a 1.3B diffusion transformer with DAC VAE and Qwen3 text conditioning. It accepts English or Chinese scene descriptions and generates up to 30 seconds at 48 kHz. The model repository declares Apache-2.0. We use the official pipeline, isolated environment, bfloat16 CUDA, deterministic seeds, 100 steps, CFG4, and disabled TorchDynamo compilation for Windows compatibility. This is synthesized scene illustration, not an authentic archival or location recording.

Windows implementation notes
MOSS runs in its own Python3.12 / PyTorch2.9 CUDA12.8 environment. Its documented torchaudio WAV writer hit a missing TorchCodec/FFmpeg DLL at export; the authored wrapper writes the generated tensor directly with soundfile instead. No synthesis change was required.
ACE runs in a separate Python3.11 environment with its own Diffusers0.37.1 and Transformers4.57.1 additions. A .pth file reads the already-installed PyTorch2.10 CUDA13 and supporting packages from the project's video environment without modifying that environment. The optional flash-attention/nano-vLLM download was stopped; inference uses ordinary PyTorch attention, no LM, no compilation. This avoids an unnecessary second large CUDA installation. Runtime logs distinguish import checks from actual generation.

Mix design
The narrator stays in the center. Scene-specific ambience occupies a wider bed, with music reduced under speech rather than buried permanently. Short sound-only openings and punctuation allow water, rain, paper, copper, cloth, bell, rope and steps to be consciously heard. Isolated effects are cued to visible actions, not placed on every edit. Tension receives quieter music and a narrower sound field; the final shared-belonging sequence opens out. Equal-power fades hide ambience loops. Master loudness and true peak are measured after assembly; numbers belong in the actual render report, not assumed here.

Boundaries
No model-generated crowd words are treated as historical dialogue. No generated bell, ship, instrument or room tone is described as a verified historical recording. Speech licensing and pronunciation evaluation are documented separately by the narration pipeline. Generation logs and per-asset JSON files record actual settings and times; performance claims in upstream project pages are not this machine's benchmark.
