FastWan DMD scheduler compatibility review
Source pinned:hao-ai-lab/FastVideo@33d81730e9fe423ab3c33dbc0451973267b80d92

Finding:the installed Wan2GP UniPC/Euler path differs from the checkpoint's official DMD sampler. This is a concrete compatibility mismatch, not proof that it caused the observed cartoon ghosting. A same-keyframe/prompt/size/seed controlled A/B remains necessary.

Official model card requests three denoising timesteps1000,757,522:
https://huggingface.co/FastVideo/FastWan2.2-TI2V-5B-FullAttn-Diffusers
It also states training at121x704x1280 and possible quality loss at other resolutions. Thus sampler mismatch is only one plausible issue when testing long or small cartoon clips.

Official recurrence:
1.Lookup sigma_t using the nearest timestep in the full1000-entry training-noise table. Construct base_sigma=1000..1 divided by1000 inFP32, then shifted_sigma=8*base_sigma/(1+7*base_sigma). The training shift8 is independent of the runtime inference shift.
2.Predict clean x0=x_t−sigma_t*v_theta(x_t,t), calculating conversion inFP64 and casting to the prediction dtype.
3.For each nonfinal step, draw fresh Gaussian noise and set x_next=(1−sigma_next)*x0+sigma_next*noise. The official sampler draws inB,T,C,H,W layout.
4.Return x0 at the final step without drawing another noise tensor.
Effective nearest-table sigmas:1.0,0.7567567229270935,0.52173912525177.

This is not the deterministic ODE Euler update x_next=x_t−(sigma_t−sigma_next)*v. Merely changing an Euler/UniPC timestep list therefore does not reproduce official DMD inference. Official DMD executes conditional passes without an extra classifier-free-guidance pass; use guidance1 for the adapter test.
