A story rooted in belonging

One peppercorn opens a journey through maritime trade, tradition, documentary evidence, living arts, education, printing and care. Pride grows from participation in Kerala’s shared history.

49 locally generated animated sequences meet 11 sourced map and evidence scenes. Malayalam narration carries the complete story without relying on the picture.

The delivery

Full H.264 film, stereo podcast MP3, Malayalam and English meaning subtitles, six chapter markers, storyboard, source scripts, research, credits and measured QA.

Honest production boundaries

Generated motion starts at 1280 × 704 / 24 fps, then is interpolated and resized for delivery. Visual checks were sparse by request. Some AI artifacts may remain. English captions are meaning summaries. The score is Kerala-inspired, not an archival or traditional-performance claim.

WAV soundtrack: −16 LUFS / −2.27 dBTP before final lossy encoding. No personal voice cloning. Model licenses and third-party image terms apply separately; this is not a blanket open-source media license.
