STORY APPROACH
The opening makes a tangible promise: one peppercorn opens a history larger than its size. The repeated objects are a peppercorn, a lamp, a document and a book. The viewer moves from curiosity to evidence, then conflict over agency, then education, craft, care and shared belonging. The final return to the peppercorn should carry new meaning rather than repeat the opening information.

Historical empowerment is earned through evidence and concrete people doing work. It does not depend on unverified antiquity, claims of pure ancestry, religious superiority, or sole credit for Kerala's development. Christian identity and Malayali belonging can coexist with respect for other communities. Institutional examples demonstrate participation; they do not establish that those institutions alone caused population-wide outcomes.

The middle and final third were revised to replace generic moral reflection with specific examples: Chavittu Natakam and working performers at Gothuruthu; women in Margamkali; CMS College1817; Baker girls' school1819; CMS press1821 and local carpenters; earlier Malayalam printing beyond Kerala; Chavara-related education and women's congregation1866; Lisie1956; Pushpagiri1959 and nursing1964; Nazrani Deepika1887; performance preservation2005; nearby religious heritage at Chendamangalam; Anna Chandy recognized by Kerala High Court. Final four scenes provide the emotional return and resolution.

STORYBOARD SOURCES AND HOW THEY ARE APPLIED

1. Pixar Animation Studios / Khan Academy, The Art of Storytelling:
https://www.khanacademy.org/computing/pixar/storytelling
https://www.khanacademy.org/computing/pixar/storytelling/story-structure/v/video1a-fine
The course connects character motivation, causal structure, visual language, boarding and feedback. Our nonfiction adaptation uses a viewer question and ordinary people as emotional anchors rather than inventing a historical protagonist's dialogue. Each scene must advance a question or reveal a consequence. The outline is an original application, not a copied Pixar formula or claim that Pixar endorses this film.

2. Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences, animation activities guide:
https://www.oscars.org/sites/oscars/files/complete_animation_activities_guide.pdf
The guide explains planning key moments and framing before costly animation, and assessing whether composition, backgrounds and movement communicate clearly. Applied here: storyboard before generation; one dominant action per shot; establish place, then show detail or consequence; clear silhouettes; eliminate objects that compete with the subject. A second shot earns its place through new information or a reaction, not an arbitrary zoom.

3. Pixar / Khan Academy, Color and Visual Language:
https://www.khanacademy.org/computing/pixar/storytelling/visual-language/v/color-visual
The lesson connects color to attention and emotional change. Applied here: warm ochre/teal for curiosity and home, cream/copper for evidence, cooler indigo for authority conflict, warmer green/gold for learning and shared agency. Colors support the scene's meaning; they are not a substitute for action.

PRODUCTION RULES
- Build a spoken animatic before locking scene duration. Keep pauses at revelations, not after every sentence.
- Each board stores a factual/emotional purpose. If a shot supplies neither, remove or redesign it.
- Use wide shots for geography or relationships, medium shots for an action, close shots for an object or reaction.
- Keep at most three foreground people, one action and three depth planes. Do not ask video models to manage a large crowd, complex hand exchange, multiple simultaneous gestures or legible writing.
- Animate simple secondary motion in background only: sail, water, rain, lamp or cloth. It must not distract from the action.
- Use real source geometry for maps. Coastlines are modern orientation, not exact ancient shoreline reconstructions. Broad routes are explanatory links, not GPS traces of ancient journeys.
- Actual documents and archival reproductions are composited assets. AI handwriting is never documentary evidence.
- Match cuts connect meaning: pepper/palm to boat/sea; rope to cooperation; book to school; opening child listening to later child learning. Do not use continuous dissolves as a replacement for editing.
- Foley must follow actions the viewer can see. Ambient beds must remain audible between speech phrases. Music should leave room for Malayalam articulation.
- Use 30fps delivery as requested. If source generation is24fps, disclose interpolation, inspect for hand/rope/face warping, and do not claim that duplicated frames are newly animated frames.

FACT CHECKS AND LIMITS

Thomas52CE: Kerala Tourism itself labels this an origin legend. Film uses the Malayalam word for tradition and explicitly states that a contemporary record establishing the exact date is unavailable. Respectful presentation does not turn the traditional arrival scene into verified footage.

Cosmas: BookIII specifically distinguishes the church at pepper-growing Male from the bishop appointed from Persia at Calliana. Male is commonly connected with Malabar; the film does not identify a precise church site, founder or arrival year.

Pattanam: the government heritage page describes trade finds and gives identification with Muziris as a possibility, not a settled equation.

SOURCE CLASSIFICATION
The JSON contains full sources and per-scene source IDs. REFLECTION means original interpretation, metaphor or ethical invitation, not an asserted historical incident. Institutional sources are used for their own founding dates and activities, not for uncritical institutional praise. Government tourism pages document traditions and places but do not supersede critical scholarship when antiquity is disputed. Primary old texts are read with their limits.
