QUALITY CHECKS
Check generated duration, waveform peak and silence, truncation/capped audio tokens, then independent local multilingual ASR for omission and pronunciation clues. ASR disagreement is not equivalent to a native-speaker judgment, especially for historical Malayalam names; final publication still benefits from a native Malayalam listening pass. Never claim human listening approval from waveform or ASR checks alone.

FULL FILM PRODUCTION
All 60 scene paragraphs were generated locally with the Malayalam (Female) preset, BF16 SDPA, batch size6 and seed4070. Original natural speech total:1107.8785seconds; generation wall time774.34seconds including model loading. No scene hit the2600audio-token safety cap. Independent Malayalam ASR was completed for all60. The shared RTX4070TiSUPER16GB held both the3B TTS and small ASR checker at about13.2GB peak observed usage, without an out-of-memory failure.

Purposeful retakes corrected omissions, repeats, dates, negations and the final emotional passage. Retakes use seed4071 and short sentence units, joined with120ms quiet gaps. Exact canonical narration remains in the script; harmless pronunciation spacing and comma variants are tracked in each scene's utterances metadata. One approved scene45 editorial line was rewritten before its final narration. Retakes did not clone any real person.

The final durations.json contains natural scene lengths, a1556-word script count, and a proposed pitch-preserving tempo factor for842.2seconds of voiced material in the900second film. Natural WAVs remain unretimed, so the director/mixer owns the final timing. Faster playback increases measured words per minute; it does not establish subjective listening quality.

Some rare names remain ambiguous in ASR, including Muziris and Gothuruth. Date and negation corrections were reviewed semantically by the Malayalam script agent. Correct names remain in canonical subtitles. These checks are useful evidence, not a native human listening certification; do not advertise the film as perfectly pronounced or independently historian-approved.
